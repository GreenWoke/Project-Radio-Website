DIY GRAFFITI STICKER SLAPS

In this workshop we will show you how to make your own DIY sticker slaps. These aren't the pinnacle of quality stickers, for that you'll need eggshell or vinyl but they can be pretty expensive! These are made with label paper so they last as long as the weather allows. This method will explain how to make about 600 of our 'Skull Stuck' designs for under $20 USD.

Steps

Step 1: Sticker Paper

You’ll need a certain type of sticker paper for this method, typically all sizes are very similar when it comes to the A4 label paper but we suggest this specific type found on Amazon.That being said, any A4 sticker label paper should work!

Step 2: Apply Design

For this example we will be using the 'Skull Stuck' design found here on the download page. This will be the design we print onto the label paper for this example. There's other designs on the downloads page for official Project Radio designs, or make your own!

Step 3: Transfer to Sticker Paper

Print your design directly on sticker paper at home using pretty much any type of printer. This design is black and white so requires no special ink colors or anything crazy. If you don't have a printer at home, take advantage of your local office supply place, friends house or wherever you can manage to print. The cost of printing in black in white is almost always next to nothing.

Step 4: Cut Out Your Stickers

Pretty self explanatory! Use scissors or a cutting blade to carefully trim your sticker.

Step 5: Set It Up (Optional)

Peel off a corner of the sticker before heading out. Fussing around trying to get the edge peeled of on these can be a bit of a struggle on the spot, peeling them a bit helps get them up faster.

Step 6: Deploy Tactically

Stick it wherever you feel it belongs: light poles, bus stops, sketchbooks, laptops. Keep it legal (or don’t i'm not ur dad). Make sure to post you getting up or spotting Project Radio in the wild in the Discord!
