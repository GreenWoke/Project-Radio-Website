Kestrel

Kestrel creates Dark Ambient music using field recordings, electronically generated drones, and both traditional and experimental instruments.

Kestrel has branched out from the previous music project, Desolo Sub Humus and has been writing, getting back into visual arts, both on paper and with other materials, including embroidery.
