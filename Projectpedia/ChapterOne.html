INFILTRATION

Following approximately one year of joint operations, Vandal and Naomi begin their biggest job yet. A targeted operation against Global Economic Architecture & Resource Supervision’s elite mech pilot program. The objective is to locate and neutralize G.E.A.R.S’s most effective mech pilot that's been wreaking havoc on civilian uprisings.

Vandal and Naomi have established operational trust through repeated successful missions, but continue to struggle with ideological and moral incompatibilities. Vandal operates from a position of empathy and rehabilitation, believing in the possibility of reform even for individuals complicit in G.E.A.R.S’s systems. Naomi, by contrast, approaches all operations with a utilitarian mindset, prioritizing efficiency and finality regardless of collateral consequences.

During the operation, the pair infiltrate a G.E.A.R.S facility and locate an individual designated Ace Pilot 03, restrained within a mech control apparatus and neurologically linked to corporate conditioning systems. Investigation reveals that the pilot is not a willing participant, but a young civilian forcibly sustained and psychologically reprogrammed to serve as a weapon.

This discovery results in a direct conflict between Vandal and Naomi. Vandal advocates for extraction and rescue, viewing the pilot as another victim of G.E.A.R.S exploitation. Naomi argues that the pilot’s past actions warrant execution, both as retribution and as a preventative measure.

Before the conflict can be resolved, the facility enters lockdown and security forces are deployed. With limited time remaining, Vandal overrides Naomi’s objections and forcibly disconnects Ace Pilot 03 from the system. When G.E.A.R.S personnel breach the facility wing, neither Project Radio nor Ace Pilot 03 are present.
