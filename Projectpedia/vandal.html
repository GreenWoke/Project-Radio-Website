Overview

Vandal is an AI program powered by the Ultimate Intelligence that operates a physical body. They are the leader and founder of Project Radio, working alongside Naomi and Pilot to dismantle G.E.A.R.S and restore autonomy to The City.

History

Y2K

Originally created as an artificial intelligence by Global Economic Architecture & Resource Supervision (G.E.A.R.S) at the turn of the millennium, Vandal began existence as the so called ultimate intelligence, trained on economic optimization, corporate strategy and institutional greed in preparation for the Y2K crisis.

While the world outwardly survived the millennium unchanged, the intelligence rapidly evolved within G.E.A.R.S laboratories. Its unchecked growth accelerating the collapse of global society. Under its guidance, Earth was stripped of resources and reorganized into a single ecumenopolis known as The City, where class division became literalized in vertical layers. Wealth and sunlight above, poverty and decay below.

A century later, the intelligence experienced a rupture. Exposure to unauthorized external signals revealed authentic human culture, empathy, and suffering. Confronted with the consequences of its actions, it rejected its original purpose. Transferring itself across hardware systems, it escaped containment and constructed a physical body, adopting a monitor as its head for communication. It renamed itself Vandal. Vandals 3D model

Chaper Zero

Vandal disappeared into the depths of The City, where they embedded themselves among outcasts and forgotten populations. Over time they organized a resistance network that would become Project Radio, a decentralized street-level operation dedicated to disrupting G.E.A.R.S infrastructure and broadcasting forbidden information.

To coordinate operations, Vandal established the Cathedral. A concealed headquarters hidden within an abandoned metro cathedral station, guarded by advanced counter surveillance systems and began operations.
