Overview

Coming from a high ranking military family and born into luxury and wealth in the higher levels of The City, Pilot became a talented Mech Operator for G.E.A.R.S at a very young age. By the time he turned 17, he became an Ace Pilot in the G.E.A.R.S ranks, having his brain reprogrammed for efficiency and unwavering cooperation after showing resistance to GEARS operations.

During the events of Infiltration and after being liberated by Project Radio, Pilot went on to work as an Operator for Project Radio in hopes of freeing The City and uncovering who they were before becoming an Ace.

History

Chapter Zero

Pilot was born into a high-ranking military family and raised in the upper levels of The City, surrounded by wealth and privilege. Demonstrating an early aptitude for electronics and mechanics, Pilot was enrolled in advanced education funded by their family. By age 15, Pilot had independently designed a functional bipedal mech prototype, attracting the attention of the G.E.A.R.S academy.

In their final year of high school, Pilot was drafted into the G.E.A.R.S academy as part of an experimental mech platoon composed of highly skilled engineers. Their unit proved effective in suppressing civil unrest and intimidating dissident populations. However, direct exposure to combat and the devastating effects of their creations instilled significant guilt and moral conflict.

Following vocalized objections to G.E.A.R.S practices, Pilot was forcibly removed from their family and integrated into the ACE PILOT system, a neurological conditioning program designed to enforce loyalty and operational efficiency. Sustained solely through life-support systems, Pilot’s brain was used to control mechs in suppression of civilian uprisings and organized resistance, effectively erasing their agency and personal identity.

By age 17, Pilot had achieved the rank of Ace Pilot, demonstrating exceptional skill in mech operation under reprogrammed obedience.

During the events of Infiltration, Pilot was located and rescued by Project Radio. Following extraction, they were placed in a rehabilitation program within the Cathedral, allowing Project Radio to evaluate their skills, psychological state, and future role in resistance operations.

After rehabilitation, Pilot elected to continue operating mechs under Project Radio’s command, contributing technical expertise and field experience to missions aimed at liberating The City from G.E.A.R.S control and reclaiming their own lost identity.
