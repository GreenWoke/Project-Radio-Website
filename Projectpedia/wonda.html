Overview

Wonda is a radio host for Project Radio, broadcasting forbidden information and underground music to the citizens of The City. Her voice is known throughout the resistance network as a beacon of hope in the darkness of G.E.A.R.S oppression.

History

Little is known about Wonda's past before she joined Project Radio. She emerged from the depths of The City, using her broadcast skills to infiltrate G.E.A.R.S communication channels.

Now she runs regular radio transmissions, playing underground music and sharing news from the resistance. Her broadcasts are a vital lifeline for many citizens who rely on her for uncensored information.
