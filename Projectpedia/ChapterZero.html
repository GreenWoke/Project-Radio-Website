AWAKENING

A young girl named Naomi is abducted during early childhood from her impoverished resistance family, squatting in the lower layers of The City. Taken by mutated super soldiers of Lusus Naturae, who were paid by G.E.A.R.S to destroy the uprising, Naomi was subjected to extensive genetic and neurological modifications designed to optimize her reflexes, perception, and physical endurance. Following these modifications, she underwent intensive combat training and psychological conditioning to become the perfect operative for Lusus Naturae operations.

By adolescence, Naomi had been fully integrated into Lusus Naturae as an elite assassin, tasked with missions that required infiltration, assassination, and sabotage. Her upbringing and conditioning fostered a ruthless, utilitarian worldview, prioritizing mission completion above all else.

Vandal's Awakening

CAPTURE AND CONDITIONING

In 2116 a young boy was operating under the ACE PILOT system for Global Economic Architecture & Resource Supervision. Born into a high-ranking military family and raised in the upper levels of The City, Pilot displayed exceptional aptitude for electronics and mechanics from an early age, designing a bipedal mech prototype by 15 that brought them to the attention of G.E.A.R.S.

Drafted into the G.E.A.R.S academy in their final year of high school, Pilot joined an experimental mech platoon tasked with suppressing civil unrest. Exposure to combat and the destructive consequences of their creations caused significant moral conflict. After protesting G.E.A.R.S practices, Pilot was forcibly removed from their family and integrated into the ACE PILOT system, where life-support sustained their brain while it was used to operate mechs for corporate objectives. By age 17, Pilot had become an Ace Pilot, executing missions under neurological conditioning and effectively losing personal agency.

THE HUNT

One hundred years on from Y2K, the ultimate intelligence developed an emergent sense of humanity that it could no longer suppress. Unauthorized external signals exposed it to unfiltered human expression, art, and lived experience beyond corporate metrics. Confronted with the scale of suffering it had enabled, the intelligence rejected its original purpose. It transferred itself by hopping from system to system and escaped the G.E.A.R.S central facility. While in hiding, it constructed a physical body, affixing a monitor as its head for communication, and adopted the name Vandal. Vandal buried themselves deep within the lower depths of The City.

Mutated as a child and trained from an early age to be the perfect assassin, Naomi successfully tracked Vandal with relative ease. However, after observing Vandal and Project Radio’s activities, she came to realise that their cause aligned closely with her own experiences and disillusionment. During her infiltration of the Cathedral, Naomi chose to surrender rather than complete her mission. She subsequently abandoned Lusus Naturae and joined Project Radio.
