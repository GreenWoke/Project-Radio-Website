Overview

Born in the shadows of The City, Naomi was the product of an illegal splicing experiment that fused feline DNA with human resilience. Her heightened reflexes, silent movement, and enhanced vision made her a natural operative long before she chose a side. When corporate enforcers wiped out her neighbourhood during a “data reclamation” raid, Naomi vanished into the city’s underbelly.

History

Captured by a violent and inhumane mutant militia, Lusus Naturae, in a data reclamation raid, Naomi was experimented on and tortured as a child, turning her from human into a feline killing machine.

After joining Project Radio prior to Chapter One, Naomi became one of their most unpredictable assets. Armed with her custom TEK-9, and her recon skills, she blends close-quarters combat with digital infiltration.
